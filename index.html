Before you reply, pay, or click

  1. Pause. Urgency is a tool. Real organizations accept "I'll call you back."
  2. Verify independently. Find the official number or site yourself - never use contact details from the message.
  3. Never pay to receive. Jobs, prizes, and parcels do not need upfront "verification" fees.
  4. Guard codes and PINs. Enter OTPs only in the official app or site you opened yourself - never share them with someone who messaged or called you first.
  5. Ask someone you trust. A two-minute second opinion beats a costly mistake.
